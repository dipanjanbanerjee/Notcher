Weather and air quality right on the notch.

### New

- **Choose what the right side of the notch shows.** In Settings › Behaviour › *Right side of the notch*, pick **Clock** (as before) or **Weather & AQI**. Weather & AQI shows the current temperature and the US air quality index for your place, in the same style as the clock. The clock shows until the weather has loaded, and again if the weather is more than 3 hours old.
- **Lighter at rest.** While the notch shows the weather and no focus timer is running, it updates less often, so it uses even less CPU.

### Your data is safe

Updating keeps your settings, clipboard history, notes, screen time and Notcher Drop files.
