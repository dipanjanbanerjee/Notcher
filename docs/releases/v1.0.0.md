The first release of Notcher: a fluid island at the top of your screen for Windows 10 and 11.

**Download `Notcher-Setup.exe` below**, run it, then move your mouse to the top of the screen.

### What's inside

- **Home:** clock, music, screen time, focus timer, system, last copy, notes and weather, in widgets you can drag around.
- **Clipboard history:** the last 200 things you copied, with search and pins. Passwords are never saved.
- **Notes:** quick notes with checklists.
- **Focus:** Pomodoro, timer and stopwatch, plus a water reminder.
- **Tools (new):** a unit converter (length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed, data, time and live currency rates) and the weather with a 5-day outlook.
- **System:** CPU, memory, disk, battery and today's screen time.
- **Drop:** send files between your phone and PC by scanning a QR code.
- Notifications from WhatsApp, Telegram and Mail pop out of the island.
- Internet speed on the taskbar, now with left and right arrows in Settings to nudge it into place.
- A new logo and a compact tab bar. Opening Settings now closes the island so it no longer covers the window.

### Good to know

- Windows SmartScreen may warn that the app isn't recognised, because Notcher isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info › Run anyway**.
- `Notcher_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` is the same installer as `Notcher-Setup.exe`; the `.msi` is for managed installs.
